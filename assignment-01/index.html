My Top 5 Travel Destinations

  1. Montemorelos, Nuevo Leon, Mexico

    The city of Montemorelos holds a special place in my life. Mountains and rivers surround the city, and it's greenery is beautiful. It's been home to my family for generations, and I visit a few times every year.

  2. Palm Springs, California

    I was born in California, and Palm Springs is unlike any other place I've ever been to. The desert landscape is stunning, and I love the warm weather. They have an aerial tramway that I recommend riding to see the desert in all it's glory.

  3. Seattle, Washington

    As someone who loves geography, Seattle is the ultimate location for beautiful views of mountains and bodies of water. Despite the constant overcast, the city has charm and grit. I recommend taking a ride on a ferry ride, you'll get the best view of their skyline from the water.

  4. Austin, Texas

    I've visited Austin several times within the last few years, and I have to say that it's such a fun city! There is so much to do and see. The city comes alive at night and it feels like home everytime I visit. If you love food, it's the place for you.

  5. Cannon Beach, Oregon

    Cannon Beach is a hidden gem in the coast of Oregon. The Haystack Rocks are extrodinary to see in person. It's also a quaint town with many local restaurants and shops. It's the perfect place for a weekend getaway, or forever home. I yearn to go back and visit.
